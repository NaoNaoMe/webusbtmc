Unlock a modular instrument

Keysight (or Agilent) U27xx modular instruments stay in “firmware update” mode when they are powered on. This tool switches them to USBTMC mode, so that the other samples can use them.

  1. Connect the instrument with a USB cable.
  2. Press Unlock and choose the instrument in the dialog of the browser.
  3. The instrument restarts. Then use it from the other samples.
