WebUSBTMCv2

Control test and measurement instruments from your browser. Choose a sample.

  • Basic I/O

    Send commands to an instrument and read the answers. A quick way to check the connection.

  • Screenshot

    Capture the screen of an oscilloscope and save it as an image.

  • Frequency response

    Measure the frequency response of a circuit with an oscilloscope. Bode and Nyquist plots.

  • DMM logger

    Record the readings of one or two DMMs over time. Save them as CSV and look at them again.

  • Speech control

    Send commands to an instrument by voice.

  • Unlock modular instruments

    Switch Keysight (Agilent) USB modular instruments from firmware update mode to USBTMC mode.
